**Expected:** `axm sync --yes --json --non-interactive` would apply a clean,
previously reviewed sync preview, consistent with the confirmation flags used
by the AXM version and pack-add operations in the same session.
**Observed:** AXM returned a usage error stating that `--yes` is unrecognized
for `axm sync` and made no change.
**Impact:** One apply attempt and one extra command were required; elapsed delay
was not measured.
**Recovery:** Reinvoke the already-previewed sync without `--yes`; task
completion remained possible.
**Detected by:** The CLI exited with a structured `usage` error.
**Observed factors:** AXM sync preview had completed with zero warnings and one
ready managed-region update; the invocation also included `--json` and
`--non-interactive`.
**Hypothesis:** Confirmation flags are command-specific and `sync` does not
share the `--yes` option accepted by other mutating AXM commands.
**Suggests:** Make the supported non-interactive apply form prominent in
command help or reject cross-command confirmation assumptions earlier.

Evidence: The rejected invocation was `axm sync --yes --json
--non-interactive`; AXM reported `Unrecognized flag: --yes in command axm sync`.
