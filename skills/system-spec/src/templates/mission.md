# Mission template

Use for why the business or product that the system serves exists: whom it
serves and what it does for them.

## Type contract

- **MIS-1** The title MUST be "<Business or product> mission".
- **MIS-2** A Mission document MUST include these sections:
  - **Mission**: whom the business or product serves and what it does for
    them, in the present tense, in one or two sentences.
- **MIS-3** A Mission MUST NOT state a time horizon, a measure or target,
  features, or how the mission is carried out.

## Suggested document

```markdown
---
type: Mission
title: <Business or product> mission
description: <The mission, in one sentence>
status: draft
---

# <Business or product> mission

## Mission

<Business or product> <does what> for <whom>.

## Open questions
## Related
```

## Writing guidance

### Mission, vision, and purpose

The mission says why the business or product exists now, the
[vision](vision.md) says the future it pursues, and a System's **Purpose**
says what one system is and does. A mission stays true when a different system
serves the business, as the profile's
[ownership tests](../references/profile.md#direction-job-or-business-requirements)
decide:

```markdown
The rental business keeps contractors working by getting them the equipment
their work needs, where and when the work needs it.
```

The rental system's purpose, letting customers find and reserve equipment and
depot staff hand it over and take it back, is one way of carrying that
mission out.

### Statement

- Name whom the mission serves in the business's own words, and link the
  glossary entry when a name needs one.
- Say what the business does for them that another business would not say of
  itself; "delight our customers" says nothing a reader can act on.
- Record only a mission that the business has agreed. A mission that is not
  agreed is an open question, as
  [record gaps instead of inventing](../references/profile.md#record-gaps-instead-of-inventing)
  requires.

### Shared missions

When several systems serve one business or product, one corpus holds the
mission and the others link it, as the profile's
[Structure](../references/profile.md#structure) requires.
