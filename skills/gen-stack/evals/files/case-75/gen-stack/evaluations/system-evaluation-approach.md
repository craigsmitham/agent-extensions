# Synthetic system evaluation approach

## Scope and objectives

The approach supports bounded change decisions for the synthetic system.

## Evaluation portfolio

Repository-native definitions and executions retain their own authority.

## Navigation and reporting

Evidence is navigable by subject and stable Requirement identity.

## Evidence and lifecycle

Results preserve provenance, unknown outcomes, and harness failures.

## Gaps and maintenance

Gaps remain visible and the approach is reviewed when its authorities change.
