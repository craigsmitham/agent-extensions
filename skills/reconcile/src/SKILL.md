---
name: reconcile
description: Reconcile a repository or a specified part by resolving unnecessary duplication and unintended divergence in its content.
---

Reconcile the requested scope: resolve unnecessary duplication and unintended
divergence among representations of the same facts, rules, or responsibilities.

Establish the intended meaning from applicable authority and evidence. Preserve
legitimate differences in scope, purpose, audience, and behavior; similarity
alone does not establish equivalence. Recency or frequency alone does not
establish authority.

Consolidate shared content where doing so reduces maintenance burden. Where
separate representations remain useful, make their relationship clear and use
appropriate means to keep them consistent.

The result should preserve required information and behavior, with evidence
supporting its coherence and any unresolved conflicts or uncertainty made
explicit.
