# Product Management knowledge bundle

Portable product management guidance for connecting strategy to product value,
customer demand, outcomes, discovery, delivery, and evidence.

Start with [Product management](src/index.md). The bundle deliberately avoids a
tracker, document hierarchy, stage model, or required organizational structure.
Consuming teams can project its concepts into their own artifacts and workflows.
