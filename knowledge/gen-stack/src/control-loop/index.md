# Control loop

OODA and change-intake guidance for interpreting Signals and Observations,
selecting authorized decisions, and taking bounded action without allowing
evidence or work items to rewrite desired state automatically. Reusable
standing models of work live in [Processes](../processes/); durable case-record
guidance lives in [Software work items](../work-items/).

- [OODA as the Gen Stack control loop](ooda-control-loop.md) - How Observe, Orient, Decide, and Act govern learning and repair across Gen Stack authorities without becoming another artifact hierarchy.
