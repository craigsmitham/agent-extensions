# Requirements

The requirement is the form a design choice takes once it must survive being
disputed, traced through change, and bound to verification. This subtree holds
the portable craft of discovering, analyzing, specifying, reviewing, changing,
and maintaining requirements, without prescribing a delivery method,
architecture framework, document form, or requirements-management tool.

Choosing the form itself belongs to [What to build](../); the problem a
requirement answers belongs to [What to solve](../../problem/). Where product
meaning stops and a normative statement begins is covered by
[Product meaning and requirements](../product-meaning-and-requirements.md).

## Model and development

- [Foundations](foundations/) - The portable model, authority and maturity,
  artifact boundaries, verification and validation, and the one-authority
  traceability model that the other sections assume.
- [Development](development/) - Turning sources into analyzed candidates:
  source discovery, candidate development, subject and level selection, and
  conflict resolution before acceptance.

## Specification and assurance

- [Authoring](authoring/) - Writing accepted or proposed obligations down:
  content contract, classification, method selection, form-specific writing
  guidance, and a compact fallback template.
- [Review](review/) - Quality review of a requirement already written, whether
  one requirement or a bounded requirement set.
- [Lifecycle](lifecycle/) - Keeping meaning and evidence intact after
  acceptance: impact analysis, change specification, and stable identity and
  lineage.

## Local use

- [Adaptation](adaptation/) - Fitting the portable model to one project's
  instructions and requirement hosts, and holding the boundary with the
  [work items](../../delivery/work-items/) that coordinate change around a
  requirement.
