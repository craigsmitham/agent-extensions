# How to learn

How do we notice what happened, keep what we learned, and retire what is stale?

Turning experience into durable, trustworthy knowledge, and removing knowledge
that has stopped being true. This section owns the feedback loop that improves
every other section. Documentation craft, meaning how a retained claim is then
expressed for a reader, stays outside this bundle in the `docs` bundle.

## Planned scope

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge authority | Which source owns a claim, in which form, and how readers reach it |
| Provenance and lifecycle | Trust, currency, admission, deprecation, and subtraction |
| Observation and evidence | Capturing what actually happened well enough to act on it later |
| Improvement practice | Retrospection and how a lesson becomes a durable change |
