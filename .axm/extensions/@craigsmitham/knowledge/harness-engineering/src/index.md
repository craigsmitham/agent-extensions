# Harness engineering knowledge

Portable knowledge for designing, evaluating, and maintaining the systems
around agents: their context, runtime, interfaces, state, feedback, and
controls. Begin with the general disciplines, then choose an application
domain, element, or guide for the work at hand.

## Foundations

- [Harness engineering foundations](foundations/) - Domain-independent
  disciplines and principles for harness and context engineering.
- [Harness engineering glossary](glossary.md) - Concise definitions for the
  disciplines, domains, topologies, environments, and elements used throughout
  the harness-engineering bundle.

## Application domains

- [Application domains](domains/) - Profiles that apply the general disciplines
  to recognizable kinds of work without treating domain assumptions as
  universal.
- [Software engineering](domains/software-engineering/) - Harness and context
  concerns for changing software across local development, repositories,
  remote workers, and distributed runtimes.

## Elements

- [Harness elements](elements/) - Portable concepts for constituents such as
  agent skills and instruction files, with domain assumptions separated into
  application profiles.

## Do

- [Guides](guides/) - Goal-oriented workflows for creating and maintaining
  harness elements; domain-specific guides live with their application domain.

## Reuse and operate

- [Patterns](patterns/) - Named structural solutions to recurring harness
  design problems, beginning with progressive disclosure.
- [Practices](practices/) - Recurring habits that keep a harness accurate and
  effective, beginning with context gardening.

## Compatibility

- [Instruction files (legacy route)](instruction-files.md) - Deprecated route
  retained for callers that used the original concept ID.
- [Former explainer routes](explainers/) - Deprecated routes retained for
  concept IDs that predate the subject-first structure.
