# Codebase Research Brief shape

Use this shape selectively. Omit sections and fields that do not apply; never
leave empty boilerplate.

```markdown
# Codebase Research Brief: <neutral topic>

**Brief status:** <Ready | Blocked — missing input>
**Review status:** <Accepted | Requested | Not required>

## Evidence Timeline
- Observed at: <time or Unknown when material>
- Reported at: <time or Unknown when material>
- Affected snapshot or environment: <material identifiers only; mark an unavailable material fact Unknown>
- Brief prepared: <time>
- Anchor verification: <time; repository, branch, commit, worktree state, and material environment or versions>

## Scope
<Current behavior or capability to investigate, without a presumed cause or solution.>

## Reported Evidence
- <Substantive reported observation or reproduction detail; omit provenance already in the timeline and label the content reported rather than verified.>

## Known Anchors
- `<verified path, symbol, subsystem, or user-visible term>`

## Human Input Gaps
- <Only unresolved facts that codebase research cannot supply. Omit when empty.>

## Research Questions

### Q1 — <short current-state label>
**Question:** <one neutral, evidence-answerable question>
**Evidence sought:** <definitions, references, runtime observations, tests, history, configuration, or authoritative external documentation>

## Boundaries
- <Intentionally excluded systems or concerns.>

## Completion Criteria
- Every question is answered, partially answered, or explicitly unresolved with the evidence searched.
- Relevant drift among reported, framing, and research snapshots is assessed.
```
