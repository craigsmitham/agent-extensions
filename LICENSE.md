for each package is the SPDX expression in its manifest.

- In the software-engineering bundle, the release-engineering material retains CC-BY-4.0. The other six disciplines retain CC-BY-SA-4.0. Sources and attribution accompany the concepts. The pack metadata and release-engineering skill use MIT. Earlier releases retain their published licenses.

  templates, and validator use MIT, while the vendored Open Knowledge Format
  specification retains its upstream Apache-2.0 license.
- All other current packages are licensed under MIT.
